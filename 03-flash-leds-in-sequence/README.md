## Flash 8 leds in sequence

Define the 8 GPIO pins, Pin0 (GPIO_PIN_0) to Pin7 (GPIO_PIN_7), for LED0 to LED7 in main.h file.

Flash every led of LED0 to LED7 in sequence.

## Process steps
Step 1: Trun on LED.

Step 2: Delay.

Step 3: Trun off LED.

Step 4: Delay.

Repeat these steps for 8 LEDs.
