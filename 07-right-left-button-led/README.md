## Pressing right button and left button to move LED light

Connect 8 LEDs for GPIO pins, PC0 to PC7.

The right button and the left button connect to GPIO pin 13 and pin 14.

## Process steps

When press the right button, the LED light move right.

Press the left button, then the LED light move left.
